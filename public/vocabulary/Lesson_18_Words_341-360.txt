Vocabulary lesson 18. Words 341 to 360.
Listen to each word, its meaning, and the examples.
Word 341. I’m certain.
Meaning: I am sure or confident.
First example: I’m certain we will succeed.
Second example: I’m certain she told the truth.

Word 342. Perseverance.
Meaning: Continued effort despite difficulties.
First example: Success requires perseverance.
Second example: Her perseverance helped her achieve her goals.

Word 343. Don’t buy it.
Meaning: Don’t believe something.
First example: He said he was sick, but I don’t buy it.
Second example: That excuse sounds fake — I don’t buy it.

Word 344. Calm and poised.
Meaning: Relaxed, confident, and in control.
First example: She remained calm and poised during the crisis.
Second example: A leader should stay calm and poised.

Word 345. Benevolent.
Meaning: Kind and willing to help others.
First example: The benevolent donor helped many families.
Second example: He is known as a benevolent leader.

Word 346. Behold.
Meaning: Look at something carefully (formal/dramatic).
First example: Behold the beauty of nature.
Second example: Behold the new product launch.

Word 347. Spectacles.
Meaning: Glasses worn to improve vision.
First example: My grandfather wears spectacles.
Second example: I can’t read without my spectacles.

Word 348. Surpass.
Meaning: To go beyond or do better than.
First example: Our sales this year surpassed last year's numbers.
Second example: She worked hard to surpass her goals.

Word 349. Nuked.
Meaning: Destroyed completely, especially with bombs or force.
First example: The city was almost nuked during the war.
Second example: He joked that his phone battery got nuked after heavy use.

Word 350. By far.
Meaning: Much more than others; clearly the most.
First example: This is by far the best restaurant in town.
Second example: She is by far the most experienced employee.

Word 351. Devastating.
Meaning: Extremely harmful or shocking.
First example: The flood had a devastating impact on farmers.
Second example: Losing the match was devastating for the team.

Word 352. Sovereignty.
Meaning: The power of a country to govern itself.
First example: Every nation protects its sovereignty.
Second example: The government emphasized national sovereignty.

Word 353. Integrity.
Meaning: Honesty and strong moral principles.
First example: A leader must have integrity.
Second example: She is respected for her integrity.

Word 354. Plunges.
Meaning: Falls or drops suddenly.
First example: The temperature plunges at night.
Second example: The stock price plunged after the news.

Word 355. Nearly.
Meaning: Almost, but not completely.
First example: I nearly missed the bus.
Second example: The project is nearly finished.

Word 356. Catastrophic.
Meaning: Causing great damage or disaster.
First example: The earthquake caused catastrophic damage.
Second example: A major system failure could be catastrophic.

Word 357. Revolted.
Meaning: Felt strong disgust or protested against something.
First example: Citizens revolted against the unfair law.
Second example: I was revolted by his rude behavior.

Word 358. Severe.
Meaning: Very serious or intense.
First example: He suffered severe injuries.
Second example: The weather conditions are severe today.

Word 359. Impunity.
Meaning: Freedom from punishment for wrongdoing.
First example: Criminals should not act with impunity.
Second example: Corruption grows when people escape with impunity.

Word 360. Staggering.
Meaning: Very surprising or shocking in size or amount.
First example: The company reported staggering profits.
Second example: The cost of the project was staggering.

End of lesson. Review the words before moving on.
