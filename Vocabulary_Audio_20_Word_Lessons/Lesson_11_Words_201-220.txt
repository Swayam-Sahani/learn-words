Vocabulary lesson 11. Words 201 to 220.
Listen to each word, its meaning, and the examples.
Word 201. Any lesser.
Meaning: Anything less than that.
First example: I won’t accept any lesser than full payment.
Second example: He deserves success, nothing lesser.

Word 202. Smirk.
Meaning: A small, smug smile.
First example: He gave a smirk after winning the argument.
Second example: She smirked as if she already knew the answer.

Word 203. Cunning.
Meaning: Clever in a tricky or sneaky way.
First example: He is a cunning businessman.
Second example: The fox is known for being cunning.

Word 204. Hypothesis.
Meaning: A possible explanation that needs proof.
First example: Scientists tested their hypothesis in the lab.
Second example: My hypothesis is that the market will grow next year.

Word 205. Envision.
Meaning: To imagine something in the future.
First example: I envision this project becoming very successful.
Second example: She envisions a better future for her company.

Word 206. Take place (correct form).
Meaning: To happen or occur.
First example: The meeting will take place tomorrow.
Second example: The event took place in Mumbai.

Word 207. Gain a better understanding.
Meaning: To understand something more clearly.
First example: Reading helped me gain a better understanding of the topic.
Second example: Talking to experts helped us gain a better understanding.

Word 208. I suppose.
Meaning: I think / I guess.
First example: I suppose he will arrive soon.
Second example: I suppose this is the best option.

Word 209. Affinity.
Meaning: Natural liking or connection.
First example: She has an affinity for music.
Second example: I feel a strong affinity towards nature.

Word 210. Abduct.
Meaning: To take someone away by force.
First example: The criminal tried to abduct the child.
Second example: The movie is about aliens who abduct humans.

Word 211. Nuisance.
Meaning: Something or someone annoying.
First example: The constant noise is a nuisance.
Second example: Mosquitoes are a big nuisance in summer.

Word 212. Seek friendship.
Meaning: To try to become friends with someone.
First example: He moved to a new city and tried to seek friendship.
Second example: She joined clubs to seek friendship with new people.

Word 213. Much more.
Meaning: A lot more than something mentioned.
First example: This job requires patience and much more.
Second example: The phone offers good performance and much more.

Word 214. Pose as human.
Meaning: Pretend to be a human.
First example: In the movie, aliens pose as humans.
Second example: The robot could pose as a human in the story.

Word 215. Gasping.
Meaning: Breathing quickly because of shock or lack of air.
First example: He was gasping after running fast.
Second example: She was gasping in surprise when she heard the news.

Word 216. Unless.
Meaning: Except if something happens.
First example: I won’t go unless you come with me.
Second example: You won’t succeed unless you work hard.

Word 217. Generally speaking.
Meaning: In most cases / usually.
First example: Generally speaking, people prefer simple solutions.
Second example: Generally speaking, exercise improves health.

Word 218. Kinda wanna.
Meaning: Informal way of saying “kind of want to”.
First example: I kinda wanna try that new restaurant.
Second example: I kinda wanna take a short break.

Word 219. Coulda woulda (shoulda).
Meaning: Talking about regrets about the past.
First example: It’s all coulda woulda now; the chance is gone.
Second example: We coulda woulda won if we practiced more.

Word 220. Let me ask you this.
Meaning: Phrase used before asking an important question.
First example: Let me ask you this, do you really enjoy your job?
Second example: Let me ask you this, what would you do in my place?

End of lesson. Review the words before moving on.
